ShipBoost Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

ShipBoost ("the App") provides a dynamic free shipping bar and cart progress indicator for Shopify merchants.

1. Information We Collect

When you install the App, we access only the store domain, theme configuration, and app display preferences (such as shipping goal amount, bar colors, and custom messages) needed to display the shipping bar on your storefront. We do not collect, store, or sell personal data of your store's end customers.

2. How We Use Information

Store settings are used solely to render your customized shipping bar and manage your ShipBoost subscription plan via Shopify Billing.

3. Data Retention & GDPR Compliance

We comply with Shopify's mandatory GDPR webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact). When you uninstall the App, your store token is revoked and store data is deleted upon Shopify's redaction request.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support, contact the ShipBoost support team through the Shopify App Store listing.
